- **wooden bench**: Small light-wood storage bench sitting directly under the basement window.
- **basement doorway**: Unfinished wood-trimmed doorway leading out of the tiled area with the red rug.
- **dark wood dresser**: Chest of drawers standing against the wall just right of the basement doorway.
- **small dark bookcase**: Low three-shelf wooden bookcase against the grey-green wall.

- **upper carpeted stairs**: Flight of beige-carpeted stairs with a wooden handrail leading up from the maroon door landing.

- **narrow wooden closet door**: Very narrow vertical wood door set in the wall between two doorways.

- **window with sheer tan curtains**: Double sliding window with white blinds framed by sheer tan curtains.

# How landmarks connect
- red bordered rug -> wooden bench: Move toward the basement doorway with the dark wood dresser on the right, then pivot left past the small dark bookcase and three framed pictures on the left wall to face the basement window and wooden bench ahead.
- wooden bench -> corner wooden shelving unit: Walk forward past the basement window and wooden bench on the left and the wood-paneled wall with door on the right to reach the corner wooden shelving unit and low wood-paneled wainscot ahead.
- corner wooden shelving unit -> basement doorway: Turn right to reverse direction, pass the wood-paneled wall with door on the left and the red bordered rug on the right, and walk straight through the basement doorway.
- basement doorway -> maroon exterior door with sidelight: After exiting through the basement doorway, immediately turn left and climb the lower carpeted stairs to the landing facing the maroon exterior door with sidelight.
- maroon exterior door with sidelight -> brass dining chandelier and table: At the landing with the maroon exterior door with sidelight on the right, turn left and climb the upper carpeted stairs toward the brass dining chandelier and table at the top.
- brass dining chandelier and table -> narrow wooden closet door: From the top of the upper carpeted stairs or moving past the round wall clock above half-wall on the right and the brass dining chandelier and table on the left, go into the narrow carpeted passage to reach the narrow wooden closet door.
- narrow wooden closet door -> bed with blue and brown quilt: With the narrow wooden closet door on the right, walk forward and turn right through the open doorway to see the bed with blue and brown quilt on the left and the window with sheer tan curtains ahead.
- bed with blue and brown quilt -> twin wood closet doors: Walk toward the window with sheer tan curtains and turn right to face the twin wood closet doors.
- twin wood closet doors -> small two-drawer nightstand: Turn left past the tall five-drawer dresser and window with sheer tan curtains, then continue looping past the twin wood closet doors on the left and the bed with blue and brown quilt on the right to the small two-drawer nightstand in the corner.
- small two-drawer nightstand -> blue striped shower curtain: Turn around past the bed with blue and brown quilt and twin wood closet doors, exit through the doorway, turn right, and step past the wooden vanity cabinet and white toilet on the left to face the blue striped shower curtain and purple hanging towels.
- blue striped shower curtain -> narrow wooden closet door: Turn right past the purple hanging towels and step out through the doorway to face the narrow wooden closet door straight ahead.
- narrow wooden closet door -> closed wood door by grey bin: Enter the doorway to the left of the narrow wooden closet door, step onto the wood-grain floor passing the carved wood bench, curtained window above bench, and black office chair and dark desk on the left, and turn right toward the closed wood door by grey bin and grey plastic trash bin.
- closed wood door by grey bin -> upper carpeted stairs: Turn right past the tall two-door wooden armoire and curtained window above bench on the right and the grey plastic trash bin on the left, exit the doorway, pass the blue striped shower curtain on the right, and walk to the top of the upper carpeted stairs on the left.
- upper carpeted stairs -> maroon exterior door with sidelight: Walk down the upper carpeted stairs to the tiled landing in front of the maroon exterior door with sidelight.
- maroon exterior door with sidelight -> yellowish wood-paneled wall: At the landing with the maroon exterior door with sidelight on the right, turn left and walk down the lower carpeted stairs, then turn around at the bottom to face the passage with the yellowish wood-paneled wall on the left.
- yellowish wood-paneled wall -> washer and dryer: With the yellowish wood-paneled wall on the left, step through the doorway on the right and turn right to see the washer and dryer on the left and the white water heater ahead.
- washer and dryer -> plaid sofa: Exit the doorway with the washer and dryer, turn left down the carpeted passage, and walk straight through the end doorway past the plaid sofa and wood-trimmed window by plaid sofa on the left.
- plaid sofa -> recessed wood-trimmed window: Turn around and walk out past the plaid sofa through the doorway, immediately turn left through the adjacent doorway past the wooden wall coat rack and closed wood closet door on the left and the bed with patterned grey bedspread on the right to reach the recessed wood-trimmed window ahead.
- recessed wood-trimmed window -> closed wood closet door: In the corner by the recessed wood-trimmed window, turn right past the bed with patterned grey bedspread to face the closed wood closet door.
- closed wood closet door -> lower carpeted stairs: Walk past the closed wood closet door on the left and exit through the doorway, turn left into the carpeted passage past the yellowish wood-paneled wall on the right, and turn right to face the lower carpeted stairs.
- lower carpeted stairs -> maroon exterior door with sidelight: Walk up the lower carpeted stairs to the landing with the maroon exterior door with sidelight on the right.
- brass dining chandelier and table -> corner CRT TV on wooden table: With the brass dining chandelier and table on the right (or behind on the left when coming from the tiled area), walk onto the beige carpet between the black leather sofa on the left and the black leather recliner on the right toward the corner CRT TV on wooden table ahead.
- corner CRT TV on wooden table -> end table with white-shaded lamp by cutout: At the corner CRT TV on wooden table, turn right and walk across the beige carpet to the corner with the end table with white-shaded lamp by cutout.
- end table with white-shaded lamp by cutout -> brass dining chandelier and table: Turn around at the end table with white-shaded lamp by cutout and walk forward between the black leather recliner and framed landscape picture above recliner on the left and the black leather sofa on the right toward the brass dining chandelier and table on the tiled floor ahead.
- brass dining chandelier and table -> white stove and black microwave: Step onto the tile floor passing the brass dining chandelier and table and curtained sliding glass door on the right and the white dishwasher and wood cabinets on the left, then turn left to face the white stove and black microwave.
- white stove and black microwave -> white dishwasher and wood cabinets: Turn right from the white stove and black microwave to face the white dishwasher and wood cabinets.
- white dishwasher and wood cabinets -> corner CRT TV on wooden table: Walk out from the counter area passing the brass dining chandelier and table on the left and the round wall clock above half-wall ahead, then turn right onto the beige carpet between the black leather sofa on the left and the black leather recliner on the right toward the corner CRT TV on wooden table.
- corner CRT TV on wooden table -> brass dining chandelier and table: Near the corner CRT TV on wooden table, turn right in a circle past the black leather recliner and framed landscape picture above recliner to face the black leather sofa and round wall clock above half-wall, then walk past the black leather recliner on the left and black leather sofa on the right toward the brass dining chandelier and table.
