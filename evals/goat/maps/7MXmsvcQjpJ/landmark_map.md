# How landmarks connect
- red armchair -> wooden dresser: With the red armchair on your left and white bed on your right, walk forward across the blue-bordered rug toward the dollhouse and wooden dresser.
- wooden dresser -> stair railing: Turn right past the wooden dresser on your left and walk through the doorway in the tree wallpaper wall to reach the stair railing and skylight.
- stair railing -> wooden desk with chair: With the stair railing on your right, turn left and pass through the open white paneled door on your left to reach the wooden desk with chair.
- wooden desk with chair -> small recessed window: Walk forward past the wooden desk with chair on your left toward the small recessed window and wooden bookshelf with file box, with the portable AC unit to your right.
- small recessed window -> stair railing: Turn around and walk back out past the wooden desk with chair and through the white paneled door to face the stair railing and green wall.
- stair railing -> open-top wooden chest: Step through the doorway adjacent to the stair railing, passing the light wooden armoire on your left, and turn around to face the open-top wooden chest on your left.
- open-top wooden chest -> carved wooden bench: Turn left from the open-top wooden chest and walk across the purple patterned rug toward the carved wooden bench and mirror above bench.
- carved wooden bench -> large window with radiator: Walk past the carved wooden bench on your right, with the dark wooden trunk and gray-covered bed to your left, toward the large window with radiator.
- large window with radiator -> dark low bookcase: With the large window with radiator on your right, walk forward and turn left along the dark low bookcase beside the gray-covered bed.
- dark low bookcase -> carved wooden bench: Turn around with the dark low bookcase on your left and face the carved wooden bench across the purple patterned rug.
- carved wooden bench -> stair railing: Walk past the carved wooden bench and exit through the doorway between the open-top wooden chest on your left and light wooden armoire on your right to reach the stair railing.
- stair railing -> narrow wooden shelves: Turn left at the stair railing and green wall, then walk down the steps toward the framed picture on yellow wall and checkered bench, turning left at the landing to face the narrow wooden shelves.
