# How landmarks connect
- wooden ladder -> storage doorway: Walk forward across the grey concrete floor with the wooden ladder on your right and the artificial tree ahead on your right, then turn left past the blue plastic bins on your left to pass through the storage doorway.
- storage doorway -> wooden ladder: Pass through the storage doorway onto the grey concrete floor and turn right with the blue plastic bins on your right to reach the wooden ladder on your left.
- storage doorway -> tall wooden bookcase: Step through the storage doorway onto the tiled floor; the tall wooden bookcase is immediately to your left against the wood-paneled wall.
- storage doorway -> white double doors: From the storage doorway on the tiled floor, walk forward with the tall wooden bookcase behind you and the wooden bar to your left to reach the white double doors on your left at the edge of the blue-painted floor.
- white double doors -> wooden stairs: Walk straight across the blue-painted floor with the white double doors on your left and the louvered bi-fold doors on your right to reach the bottom of the wooden stairs.
- wooden stairs -> louvered bi-fold doors: Come down the wooden stairs onto the blue-painted floor and turn left to pass through the open louvered bi-fold doors.
- louvered bi-fold doors -> white upright appliance: Pass through the louvered bi-fold doors toward the utility sink ahead, passing the blue ironing board on your right, then turn left to walk between the water heater on your left and the washer and dryer on your right toward the white upright appliance.
- white upright appliance -> louvered bi-fold doors: Turn around from the white upright appliance, walk between the washer and dryer on your left and the water heater on your right, then turn right past the utility sink on your left and the blue ironing board on your right to exit through the louvered bi-fold doors.
- louvered bi-fold doors -> wooden door: Exit through the louvered bi-fold doors onto the blue-painted floor with the white double doors ahead on your right, then turn left onto the tiled floor and walk past the wooden bar on your left to the closed wooden door.
- wooden door -> storage doorway: With the closed wooden door on your left, turn left across the tiled floor to face the storage doorway, with the tall wooden bookcase just to its right.
- wooden ladder -> small high windows: Walk straight down the grey concrete floor with the wooden ladder and metal storage shelves on your left and the blue plastic bins and white shelving unit on your right toward the small high windows in the far white cinderblock wall.
- small high windows -> shop vacuum: From the far white cinderblock wall under the small high windows, turn around and look down the grey concrete floor between the white shelving unit on your left and the metal storage shelves on your right toward the shop vacuum and artificial tree at the far end.
