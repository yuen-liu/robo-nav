- sliding glass door with green valance -> doorway with white door: Turn around past the potted tree, walk forward with the dining table with white chairs on your left and mirrored wall on your right, and turn right before the mint armchair.
- doorway with white door -> window with vertical blinds: Pass through the doorway with white door and walk straight between the bed with pink throw on your left and the long white dresser with mirror on your right.
- window with vertical blinds -> corner vanity with mirrors: Turn around past the tv on white chest and doorway with white door, then walk down the narrow passage with the white desk with black chair and open closet with shelves on your right and the sliding pocket door on your left.
- corner vanity with mirrors -> white bathtub: Continue straight past the corner vanity with mirrors through the doorway ahead.
- white bathtub -> doorway with white door: Turn around between the white tiled shower and white bathtub, walk back out past the open closet with shelves on your left toward the bed with pink throw, and turn left between the bed and the long white dresser with mirror.
- doorway with white door -> front door with arched window: Exit the doorway with white door, turn left, and walk across the carpet with the mint armchair and cream sofa on your right and the dining table with white chairs and doorway with blue mat on your left onto the white tiled floor area.
- front door with arched window -> white counter: With the front door with arched window behind you, follow the white tiled floor area past the small glass console table and arched wall cutout with vase on your left and the closed white door and patterned armchair on your right.
- white counter -> tan draped armchair: Walk forward past the brown fur rug and the white counter on your left and the patterned armchair on your right into the carpeted area.
- tan draped armchair -> passage doorway: Pass the tan draped armchair and mirrored wall above white cabinets on your right to enter the passage doorway ahead.
- passage doorway -> bed with turquoise cover: Walk forward along the patterned runner rug past the doorway with the black office chair and bed with pink cover on your right to reach the doorway at the end of the passage.
- bed with turquoise cover -> white vanity cabinet: Turn around and walk back along the patterned runner rug toward the tan draped armchair, then turn right before exiting the passage.
- white vanity cabinet -> glass shower stall with grab bar: Step past the white vanity cabinet and turn right, with the white toilet and exterior door with blinds on your left.
- glass shower stall with grab bar -> tan draped armchair: Step back out into the passage with the black office chair to your left, and walk straight out through the passage doorway.
- tan draped armchair -> white built-in wall oven: From the carpeted area near the tan draped armchair, walk across the white tiled floor between the white counter on your right and the round table with rolling chairs and white refrigerator on your left.
- white built-in wall oven -> round table with rolling chairs: Turn around near the white built-in wall oven and white dishwasher and walk back with the white counter on your left and the white refrigerator on your right.
