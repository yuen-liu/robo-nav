# Landmarks
- **bed with circular wall art**: Large bed with gray patterned cover, dark headboard, and round metallic wall art above it.
- **horizontal slatted window**: Wide, short window high on the wall with dark horizontal blinds.
- **white paneled door**: Standard white 6-panel interior doors found along the narrow passages and doorways.
- **bed with recessed wall art**: Bed with purple/gray cover and white throw, next to a wall with a rectangular recessed niche containing abstract art.
- **recessed wall niche with abstract art**: Horizontal rectangular cutout in the white wall holding a blue and brown abstract canvas.
- **wood-paneled alcove with live-edge shelves**: Alcove lined with vertical reddish-brown wood planks and two thick, natural-edge wooden shelves.
- **red-cushioned bench under window**: Ornate wooden bench or small table with a red cushion, sitting against a wall under windows.
- **dining table with white-backed chairs**: Dark wood dining table surrounded by six dark-framed chairs with white upholstered backs and seats.
- **bar counter with two white stools**: Wall cutout counter with two tall, white, tufted barstools on one side and a white peninsula counter with a glass-front end cabinet and suspended upper cabinets on the other.
- **potted leafy plant**: Large green leafy houseplant in a round brown basket planter, sitting in a corner.
- **blue-green abstract painting**: Medium-sized canvas painting with teal, blue, white, and orange brushstrokes on the wall near the potted plant.
- **white paneled sliding door**: Wide white paneled barn-style door on a metal track next to a step down in the wood floor.
- **teal sofa with horse painting**: Light teal upholstered sofa with nailhead trim and white/furry throw pillows, beneath a dark painting of white horses.
- **dark brown tufted armchairs**: Pair of dark brown leather or fabric armchairs with nailhead trim and a rectangular mirror on the wall above them.
- **corner brick hearth with black stove**: Corner hearth area with multi-colored brick backing and a black cast-iron wood stove with a vertical stovepipe.
- **tall potted cactus in corner**: Tall green columnar cactus in a dark pot standing in the corner between two windows.
- **smaller teal loveseat**: Two-seater teal sofa with dark pillows, positioned under a wide window.
- **recessed wall niche with small sculpture**: Vertical rectangular wall cutout with a small light at the top and a brown abstract sculpture inside, between two doorways.
- **rectangular ceiling skylight**: Long rectangular recessed opening in the ceiling above the dining table.
- **dark sofa with teal pillows**: Dark brown sofa with light teal throw pillows and a monochrome abstract painting on the wall above it.
- **pair of blue armless chairs**: Two dark blue upholstered armless accent chairs with dark wooden legs, sitting side by side.
- **interior window above blue chairs**: Wide rectangular interior glass window set into the white wall above the blue chairs, looking toward the dining table.
- **dark wood console table with striped painting**: Dark wooden two-tier console table beneath a large horizontal canvas painting with teal, orange, and white stripes.
- **gray and white chevron rug**: Area rug with a gray and white zigzag chevron pattern on the wood floor under a small glass coffee table.
- **wooden door with glass panels**: Reddish-brown wooden door with two vertical decorative glass panels in its upper half.
- **curved window bench with teal pillows**: Built-in white curved bench seat in a bay window alcove, topped with teal throw pillows.
- **white refrigerator and cabinets**: Tall white double-door refrigerator surrounded by white paneled cabinetry and drawers.
- **white clawfoot bathtub and blue tile floor**: White freestanding bathtub against a white wall with blue-and-white patterned accent tiles, sitting on a bright royal-blue tiled floor.
- **white vanity sink with dark-framed mirror**: Small white cabinet sink with a potted plant on the counter and a dark brown rectangular mirror on the tiled wall above.
- **bed with slatted wood headboard**: Large bed with a gray patterned bedspread, dark wooden headboard with square cutouts, and a horizontal window above the headboard.
- **shuttered window with dark louvers**: Square window with a white frame and dark louvered interior shutters, next to a small blue-and-gray abstract painting.
- **square black metal wall grille**: Decorative square black wrought-iron scrollwork piece mounted high on the white wall next to the doorway.
- **dark wood nightstand with white lamp**: Small dark wooden bedside table with a white shaded lamp in the corner next to the bed.
- **wood-paneled cedar closet with shelves**: Small walk-in alcove lined with horizontal reddish-brown wood planks and wooden shelving.
- **teal towels and toilet with starfish painting**: Two light teal towels hanging on a white wall under a small window, next to a white toilet with a blue starfish painting above it.
- **white double wall oven**: Built-in white double oven set into tall white cabinetry, with a patterned dish towel hanging on the lower handle.
