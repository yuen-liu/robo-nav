- **staircase with dark treads**: Staircase with white risers, dark wood treads, and a dark wood banister.
- **long dark wood table with chairs**: Dark wood table surrounded by dark tufted chairs on a light rug.

- **dark console table**: Ornate dark wood console table with turned legs against the blue-grey wall near the dark front door.
- **white French door**: White door with multiple glass panes opening into a carpeted area near the dark front door.
- **sofa with purple and yellow throw**: Dark grey sofa draped with a purple, yellow, and white blanket.
- **TV on dark slatted dresser**: Flat-screen TV sitting on a dark wood horizontal-slatted dresser in front of double windows.
- **white fretwork cabinet**: Small distressed white cabinet with geometric fretwork doors next to a window with grey curtains.
- **square mirror with autumn wreath**: Large dark-framed square mirror with a round autumn-leaf wreath hung in the center, above white wainscoting.
- **stainless steel refrigerator**: Double-door stainless steel refrigerator surrounded by white cabinets.
- **stainless steel stove**: Stainless steel range with a white towel on the handle and a stainless steel microwave mounted above it.

- white fireplace with wreath -> staircase with dark treads: Walk forward between the white fireplace with wreath on your left and the grey sectional sofa on your right onto the dark wood floor, then turn right.
- staircase with dark treads -> alcove with black door and white door: Walk forward with the staircase with dark treads on your right and the long dark wood table with chairs on your left, then turn right immediately past the stairs.

- long dark wood table with chairs -> white French door: Walk past the white wainscoting wall on your left toward the black shoe bench and dark front door, then turn left past the dark console table to pass through the white French door.
- white French door -> sofa with purple and yellow throw: Step through the white French door onto the beige carpet; the sofa with purple and yellow throw is immediately on your left opposite the TV on dark slatted dresser.
- sofa with purple and yellow throw -> white French door: Turn around with the sofa with purple and yellow throw on your right and walk straight to the white French door.
- white French door -> long dark wood table with chairs: Step through the white French door onto the dark wood floor and turn right, passing the dark console table on your left.
- long dark wood table with chairs -> square mirror with autumn wreath: Walk forward between the white wainscoting wall on your left and the long dark wood table with chairs on your right, then turn right past the white fretwork cabinet.
- square mirror with autumn wreath -> stainless steel refrigerator: Turn around to face the staircase with dark treads in the distance, then turn right into the narrow passage beside the white wainscoting wall and walk straight.
- stainless steel refrigerator -> white counter island: Turn left around the stainless steel refrigerator and walk between the white counter island on your left and the stainless steel stove on your right.
- white counter island -> grey sectional sofa: Walk straight past the white counter island on your left toward the back of the grey sectional sofa.
- grey sectional sofa -> covered dog crate: With the back of the grey sectional sofa on your left and the white counter island behind you, walk straight toward the sliding glass door and covered dog crate.
