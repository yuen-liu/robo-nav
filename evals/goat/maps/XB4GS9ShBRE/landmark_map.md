# Landmarks
- **dark-headboard-bed**: Large bed with dark wooden headboard and light patterned quilt
- **fluffy-white-rug**: Large white shaggy rug on hardwood floor next to the dark-headboard bed
- **tall-dark-dresser**: Tall dark brown wooden dresser or armoire near the doorway
- **black-nightstand**: Dark nightstand with a small lamp next to the dark-headboard bed
- **sheer-curtain-window**: Window with white sheer curtains next to the black nightstand
- **grey-dresser-with-plant**: Greyish-blue wooden chest of drawers with a potted plant on top
- **six-panel-door-to-railing**: White six-panel door separating the hardwood floor area from the upper stair railing
- **upper-stair-railing**: Wooden banister with spindles along the upper carpeted passage
- **teal-pillow-bed**: Bed with wooden headboard and bright teal pillows, straight ahead from the top of the stairs, in an area with a tied-back sheer window curtain
- **dark-bedding-bed**: Bed with dark grey comforter, tufted grey headboard, and a window beside it
- **framed-easel-print**: Dark-framed picture showing an easel on the wall above the stair landing
- **carpeted-winding-stairs**: Beige carpeted stairs that wind with a triangular landing
- **stair-bottom-door**: White six-panel door at the bottom of the carpeted stairs
- **wooden-dining-table**: Rectangular medium-wood table with carved wooden chairs on hardwood floor
- **dark-wood-corner-cabinet**: Dark stained wooden cabinet with open upper shelves at the corner of the tiled area
- **stainless-stove**: Stainless steel range and hood next to wooden cabinets
- **red-pillow-sofa**: Light beige sofa with three bright red throw pillows
- **grey-ottoman**: Square grey upholstered ottoman in front of the beige sofa
- **wide-double-window**: Large two-pane window on the wall perpendicular to the beige sofa
- **accent-chair-red-pillow**: Light patterned armless accent chair with a red pillow
- **round-wall-decor**: Dark circular wall art hanging between the sofa area and wooden table
- **tiger-print**: Framed picture of a tiger face on the wall near the wooden table
- **metal-butterfly-art**: Dark metal wall sculpture of a butterfly and leaves above two wooden steps
- **two-wood-steps**: Two hardwood steps leading down to a dark-tiled entry area
- **double-glass-entry-door**: White exterior door with two vertical glass panels and a sidelight
- **vanity-with-mirror**: White cabinet vanity with beige top and dark-framed mirror inside a small area with blue walls
- **white-toilet**: White toilet opposite the vanity in the blue-walled area
- **sloped-stair-ceiling**: Low angled white ceiling over a narrow enclosed staircase
- **white-tiled-tub**: White bathtub with white square wall tiles
- **dark-tall-chest**: Dark brown five-drawer chest next to the tufted-headboard bed
- **ceiling-fan-light**: Three-blade wooden ceiling fan with central light fixture
- **flower-wall-art**: Large metal wireframe flower decoration on the grey wall above the teal-pillow bed
- **wooden-nightstand-lamp**: Small brown two-drawer wooden nightstand with a white-shaded lamp next to the teal-pillow bed
- **sheer-curtain-window-2**: White-trimmed window with a tied-back sheer white curtain next to the teal-pillow bed
- **wall-mounted-tv-shelf**: Dark flat-screen TV mounted on the wall above a white floating shelf with a vertical cord cover
- **small-wooden-buffet**: Small medium-brown wooden cabinet with two drawers and two doors beneath the round wall decor
- **tv-on-wooden-console**: Black flat-screen TV sitting on a long rustic wooden media console with glass-front doors
- **wooden-kitchen-cabinets**: Medium-brown stained wooden upper and lower cabinets with white countertop and backsplash
- **drop-leaf-table**: Small dark wood round drop-leaf table with carved wooden chairs under a small framed mirror
- **sliding-glass-patio-door**: Sliding glass door with a vertical white sheer curtain leading out to a wooden deck with a blue patterned rug
- **floral-canvas-prints**: Two colorful unframed canvas paintings of flowers on the light blue wall near the sliding glass door

# The recorded walk
1. Start by the dark-headboard-bed and walk across the fluffy-white-rug past the tall-dark-dresser toward the black-nightstand and sheer-curtain-window, turning right to face the grey-dresser-with-plant.
2. Turn left and exit through the six-panel-door-to-railing onto the carpeted passage beside the upper-stair-railing, passing the doorway to the teal-pillow-bed on the left.
3. Turn right by the upper-stair-railing and walk down the carpeted-winding-stairs past the framed-easel-print and stair-bottom-door onto the hardwood floor facing the wooden-dining-table.
4. Walk forward with the wooden-dining-table on the left and pass the dark-wood-corner-cabinet into the tiled area by the stainless-stove.
5. Step back onto the hardwood floor past the wooden-dining-table, walk toward the wide-double-window, and turn right to face the grey-ottoman, red-pillow-sofa, accent-chair-red-pillow, and round-wall-decor.
6. Turn right and walk past the wooden-dining-table and tiger-print on the left to face the stair-bottom-door and carpeted-winding-stairs.
7. Turn left past the tiger-print and walk down the two-wood-steps beneath the metal-butterfly-art, passing the vanity-with-mirror on the right to reach the double-glass-entry-door.
8. Turn around at the double-glass-entry-door, pass the white-toilet and metal-butterfly-art on the left, and climb the two-wood-steps back toward the wooden-dining-table.
9. Go down the narrow enclosed staircase under the sloped-stair-ceiling, then turn around and return to the main level.
10. Pass the wooden-dining-table on the right and stair-bottom-door on the left, and climb the carpeted-winding-stairs alongside the upper-stair-railing toward the teal-pillow-bed.
11. At the top of the carpeted-winding-stairs, turn right to face the six-panel-door-to-railing and dark-headboard-bed with the white-tiled-tub on the right.
12. Turn left and walk into the area under the ceiling-fan-light with the dark-bedding-bed ahead and dark-tall-chest on the right.
13. Turn left and walk into the adjacent area past the teal-pillow-bed and flower-wall-art on the left to the wooden-nightstand-lamp and sheer-curtain-window-2.
14. Turn around, walk past the teal-pillow-bed on the right, and cross straight into the area with the dark-bedding-bed, passing the wall-mounted-tv-shelf on the left.
15. Turn around and walk out past the dark-tall-chest and dark-bedding-bed on the left and wall-mounted-tv-shelf on the right toward the upper-stair-railing and dark-headboard-bed.
16. Turn left along the upper-stair-railing and walk down the carpeted-winding-stairs past the framed-easel-print.
17. At the bottom of the stairs, walk forward between the wooden-dining-table and tiger-print on the right and the accent-chair-red-pillow, grey-ottoman, and red-pillow-sofa on the left toward the round-wall-decor and small-wooden-buffet.
18. Turn around to face the carpeted-winding-stairs with the wooden-dining-table on the left and accent-chair-red-pillow and tv-on-wooden-console on the right.
19. Turn right, walk past the tv-on-wooden-console on the left and grey-ottoman on the right to the wide-double-window, and turn right to face the red-pillow-sofa.
20. Turn around, walk across the hardwood floor past the wooden-dining-table on the left, and continue past the stainless-stove, wooden-kitchen-cabinets, and drop-leaf-table on the left and floral-canvas-prints on the right to the sliding-glass-patio-door.
